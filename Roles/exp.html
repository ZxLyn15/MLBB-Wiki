Role Overview

The EXP Laner is a durable frontline hero or a frontline damage dealer who gains extra experience from the EXP Lane. This role focuses on winning lane trades, creating pressure through split pushing, and initiating fights for the team. EXP Laners are often the first to engage in team fights while protecting their teammates from enemy threats.

Primary Responsibilities

Win Your Lane

Control the EXP Lane by trading efficiently and preventing the enemy EXP Laner from gaining an advantage.

Initiate Team Fights

Use your durability and crowd control to start favorable fights for your team.

Apply Side Lane Pressure

Split push when appropriate to force enemies to defend and create space for your teammates.

EXP Lane Power Spike

The EXP Lane power spike occurs during the early game when the EXP Laner can establish lane dominance and secure objectives. Focus on winning trades,
maintaining lane control, and pressuring the enemy to gain an advantage. Especially the first major objective (Turtle) is where the experience lane is most important.
The early game is crucial for setting the tone of the match and ensuring your team has a strong foundation for the mid and late game.

Often Used Battle Spells

Flicker

Provides mobility for engaging or escaping during team fights.

Sprint

Improves chasing potential and allows better repositioning.

Vengeance

Reflects damage while reducing incoming damage, making it ideal for durable EXP Laners.

Situational Battle Spell

Petrify

Petrify is used for heroes whos skills are based on crowd control. It can be used to stop enemies from escaping, make hard skills easier to hit or to initiate fights.

Often Used Emblems

Fighter Emblem

Balanced offense and durability for extended fights, used for heroes that need to survive and deal damage over time.

Assassin Emblem

Useful for burst-oriented EXP heroes that rely on quick eliminations.

Tank Emblem

Improves survivability for frontline heroes. Ideal for frontline heroes that focus on durability and sustained team fights.

Strengths

  • Durable during team fights.
  • Strong lane sustain.
  • Excellent at initiating fights.
  • Can pressure side lanes through split pushing.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Watch the minimap before pushing.
  • Don't overextend without vision of the enemies position.
  • Join important objectives like Turtle and Lord.
  • Know when to split push and when to group for teamfights.

Tips for Beginners

  • Learn your hero's power spikes.
  • Practice trading without taking unnecessary damage.
  • Rotate only after clearing your lane to avoid being behind on experience and your turret being destroyed.
  • Protect your damage dealers such as the marksman and mage during a team fights.

Note: The Experience Lane can either be at the top lane or bottom lane.
