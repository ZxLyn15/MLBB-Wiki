Overview

The Jungler is one of the most influential roles in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. Instead of staying in a single lane, the Jungler focuses on defeating jungle monsters, securing buffs, Turtle, and Lord, while rotating across the map to assist teammates through timely ganks. A skilled Jungler can create advantages for the entire team by controlling objectives and maintaining a strong farming pace.

Basic Jungle Rotation

Early Game Rotation

A common Jungle rotation begins with either the Purple Buff or Orange Buff. Many players start on the buff closest to the EXP Lane so they can reach Level 4 and rotate to the Gold Lane for an early gank.

  • Purple Buff Start: Clear Purple Buff, rotate to the Red Buff side, clear nearby camps, then prepare for a gank.
  • Orange Buff Start: Clear Orange Buff first, rotate toward Purple Buff while clearing jungle camps.
  • Save Retribution whenever possible to secure the Lithowanderer or important objectives.

Farming Priorities

  • Prioritize jungle camps over lane minions.
  • Avoid taking Mid Lane minions before your Mid Laner reaches Level 4.
  • After around 5 minutes, continue prioritizing Buffs, Turtle, and Lord while helping push lanes.

Objective Control

  • Secure Buffs consistently.
  • Contest Turtle whenever possible.
  • Use Retribution to secure Turtle and Lord.
  • Look for gank opportunities after clearing camps.

Important Tips

  • Stay hidden before ganking.
  • Track the enemy Jungler on the minimap.
  • Use pings to communicate with teammates.
  • Efficient farming is the key to staying ahead.

Recommended Battle Spell

Retribution

Retribution is essential for every Jungler because it allows faster jungle clearing and helps secure Turtle and Lord.

Recommended Emblems

Assassin Emblem

Provides additional mobility and damage, making it the preferred emblem for many Jungle heroes.

Marksman Emblem

Grants attack speed and sustained damage, making it suitable for Junglers that rely on basic attacks to farm fast.

Fighter Emblem

Offers balanced offense and durability, ideal for Fighter Junglers that engage in extended fights.

Strengths

  • Excellent objective control.
  • Can influence every lane.
  • High carry potential.
  • Fast rotations around the map.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Falling behind in farm greatly reduces your impact.
  • Always watch objective timers.
  • Avoid unnecessary fights before objectives.
  • Be aware of enemy jungle invasions.

Tips for Beginners

  • Practice efficient jungle pathing.
  • Look at the minimap every few seconds.
  • Help lanes after clearing camps.
  • Prioritize objectives over chasing kills.
