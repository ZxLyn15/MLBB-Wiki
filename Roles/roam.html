Role Overview

The Roamer is responsible for protecting teammates, providing vision around the map, and initiating team fights. Unlike other roles, Roamers do not stay in one lane. Instead, they constantly rotate to assist allies, secure objectives, and create advantages through crowd control and smart positioning.

Primary Responsibilities

Protect Teammates

Absorb damage, peel enemies away from allies, and provide utility throughout the match.

Control Vision

Check bushes, provide information, and keep teammates safe from ambushes.

Initiate Team Fights

Use crowd control to start favorable engagements for your team.

Map Awareness

Roamers should constantly watch the minimap and rotate between lanes to assist teammates. Check bushes before your allies enter them,
provide vision around Turtle and Lord, and communicate enemy positions through pings. Good map awareness often wins games before team fights even begin.

Strengths

  • Excellent crowd control.
  • Protects teammates effectively.
  • Controls vision around objectives.
  • Can influence every lane.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Always check bushes before teammates.
  • Protect your damage dealers.
  • Rotate constantly around the map.
  • Contest Turtle and Lord with your team.

Tips for Beginners

  • Do not stay in one lane for too long.
  • Use pings to communicate with teammates.
  • Engage only when allies can follow up.
  • Focus on helping your team instead of chasing kills.

Note: A good Roamer constantly rotates across the map, helping teammates while providing vision and protecting important objectives.
