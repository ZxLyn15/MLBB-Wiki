EMBLEMS

Emblems are hero enhancements that grant additional buffs or effects as well as bonus attributes. In the preparation tab or during the hero pick phase, players can choose their preferred emblem sets. Currently, there are 7 types of emblems, each with its own set of bonuses.

Standard talents and core talents are the two of talents. Players can choose additional effects for their chosen hero via talents, providing them with an advantage with the suitable configurations; however, they can only choose two standard talents and one core talent. Currently, there are 16 types of standard talents and 8 types of core talents, each with its own set of bonuses.

Common Emblem

An balanced emblem that provides Adaptive Attack, HP, and Hybrid Regen. It is suitable for a wide variety of heroes and serves as a flexible option when no specialized emblem is needed.

Attributes

Hybrid Regen +12.00
HP +275.00
Adaptive Attack +22.00

+10% Attack Speed

+10% move speed in jungle/river
(halved in hero combat)

Seasoned Hunter

+15% damage to Lord/Turtle
(halved vs creeps)

Core Talents

Impure Rage

Dealing damage with skills deals 4% of target's Max HP extra Adaptive Damage and restores 2% Mana on hit. (If the hero does not use Mana, restores 1% HP instead). The extra Adaptive Damage dealt to non-hero units is up to 120. Cooldown: 5 seconds.

Quantum Charge

Dealing damage with Basic Attacks increases Movement Speed by 30% for 1.5s and restores 75-180 HP (scales with level). Cooldown: 10 seconds.

Temporal Reign

Dealing damage with skills reduces the target's Movement Speed by 30% for 1.5s and restores 75-180 HP (scales with level). Cooldown: 10 seconds.

War Cry

The next Ultimate cast decreases other active skills' remaining cooldowns by 1.5 times within 4 seconds. This effect is extended by 2 seconds after getting hero kills or assists. (This only applies to the hero themselves. Cooldown: 20 seconds.)

Assassin Emblem

An emblem made for heroes that relies on dealing high damage, either from suprise attacks, ambushes, or pure burst damage to eliminate targets.

Attributes

Rupture

Gain 5 Adaptive Penetration. Gain Physical Penetration if extra Physical Attack is higher than extra Magic Power; otherwise, gain Magic Penetration.

Master Assassin

If there is only one enemy hero nearby, damage dealt to them is increased by 7%.

Core Talents

Killing Spree

When damaging heroes below 30% HP, instantly recover 15% of Lost HP and gains 20% Movement Speed. Additional Effect: Reset cooldown upon killing an enemy hero. Duration: 3 seconds. Cooldown: 30 seconds.
