BATTLE SPELLS

Battle Spells are special abilities that every hero can equip before a match. Choosing the right spell can improve mobility, survivability, damage, or utility.

Explore Battle Spells
Home arrow_forward_ios Battle Spells

Overview

Battle Spells provide heroes with additional abilities that can greatly influence the outcome of a match. Every spell has its own unique purpose, ranging from dealing damage and escaping danger to supporting allies and controlling objectives. Selecting the appropriate spell depends on the hero, role, and overall team strategy.
Players can equip a Battle Spell before the start of a match. Mastering Battle Spells makes the player much stronger. Spells are something that the player can use as an additional ability for their hero in-game. A player can select one battle spell while in the pregame lobby. They cannot be reset or changed once the match has begun.

Retribution Variants

After purchasing a Jungling Blessing, Retribution evolves into one of three variants depending on the blessing selected.

Ice Retribution

When cast on an enemy hero, deals 100 True Damage and steals 52-80 Movement Speed (scales with level) for 3s.

Flame Retribution

When cast on an enemy hero, deals 100 True Damage and steals 71-120 Physical Attack and Magic Power (scales with level) for 3s.

Bloody Retribution

When cast on an enemy hero, deals 100 True Damage and steals HP equal to 300 + 24% of the caster's extra HP for 3s.
